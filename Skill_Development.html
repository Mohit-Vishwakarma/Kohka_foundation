WORKSHOPS & TRAINING

Lack of employment and livelihood is a crisis plaguing rural India, especially villages and districts like Seoni, which do have a industrial investment.

With India's labour force growing expediently, Kohka Foundation has set up skill development and training programs targeted everyone from towards financially unindependant woman to school students.

This will not only ensure a form of livelihood but will also safegaurd India's rural population with having employable skills, readying them for urban professional landscapes.
